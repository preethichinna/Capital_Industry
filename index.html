ABOUT CAPITAL INDUSTRIES

Engineering made for real work.

Capital Industries manufactures industrial tanks, transport equipment and vehicle bodies for customers with practical, demanding requirements.

From petroleum tankers and underground storage tanks to containers, trailers, water tanks, automobile bodies, lorry cabins and mobile dispensers, the company brings a broad range of fabrication work together under one roof.

Explore our capabilities →

OUR VISION

Our vision is to pioneer excellence in fabrication and aspire to become a global leader in manufacturing petroleum tanks, containers, trailers, water tanks, and automobile bodies by delivering high-quality products and contributing to a sustainable future.

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to provide outstanding quality in manufacturing petroleum tanks, cabins, containers, and automobile bodies. We aim to exceed customer expectations through durable, reliable products, continuous innovation, environmental responsibility, and long-term customer satisfaction.

Rohan S Kumar · Proprietor
